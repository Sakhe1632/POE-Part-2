About Artisan's Hearth Bakery

Our Story

Artisan's Hearth Bakery was founded in 2016 by master baker Elias Van der Merwe. After years of training in traditional European bakeries, Elias returned to South Africa with a dream: to bring authentic, handcrafted bread to his local community.

What started as a small home-based bakery has grown into a beloved neighbourhood establishment, known for its commitment to quality, tradition, and community.

Today, we continue to bake with the same passion and dedication that Elias brought to the very first loaf. Every batch is made by hand, using recipes that have been perfected over generations.

Our Mission

To bring the joy of authentic, handcrafted bread and pastries to our community, using the finest local ingredients and time-honoured techniques.

Our Vision

To be a beloved community hub where the simple pleasure of good food is shared and celebrated.
