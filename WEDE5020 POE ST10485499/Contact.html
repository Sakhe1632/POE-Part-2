Find Us

Visit our bakery in the heart of Pretoria. We're easily accessible and have limited parking available.

Getting Here

By Car

We're located on Main Street, just off the N1 highway. Take the Main Street exit and travel east for approximately 2km.

By Public Transport

The nearest bus stop is 200m from our location on Main Street. Several taxi routes also pass nearby.

Parking

We have a small parking lot available for customers, with additional street parking available nearby.
